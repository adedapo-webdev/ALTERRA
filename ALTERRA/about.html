01 / OUR STORY

Real estate,
reimagined.

ALTERRA Estates & Realty is a real estate brand built on the belief that finding, owning and investing in property should be a more thoughtful experience.

We bring together property sales, rentals, property management, advisory and real estate investment opportunities under one professional approach.

With a focus on the Nigerian property market, our aim is to connect individuals, families, investors and businesses with opportunities that align with their needs and ambitions.

At ALTERRA, we believe that every property represents more than a physical space. It represents a decision, an opportunity and a future.
